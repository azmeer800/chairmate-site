Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Chairmate ("Chairmate", "we", "us") provides an appointment booking app for tattoo studios, pet groomers, and their clients. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Chairmate, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture with us so we can create and secure your account.
  • Studio information (for studio owners). Studio name, address, phone number, business hours, services and prices, staff details, and portfolio images you choose to publish.
  • Booking information. When a client books, we collect the client's name, phone number, the service and time chosen, and any notes the client adds. For tattoo bookings, a signed consent form may be stored with the booking. For pet grooming, clients may save pet profiles (pet name, breed, and care notes).
  • Basic technical information. Information needed to run and secure the service, such as sign-in activity and error logs.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, including Google Sign-In.
  • To create, confirm, and manage bookings, and to show studios their schedule and client bookings.
  • To send booking notifications and reminders.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the service, and to prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

How information is shared

  • With the studio you book with. A studio can see the booking information its own clients provide, so it can provide the service. Studios cannot see other studios' data.
  • With service providers. We use Supabase to host our database and authentication, and Google for optional sign-in. Payment processing, where offered, is handled by payment processors; Chairmate does not collect or store full payment card numbers, and payments from clients go to the studio, not to Chairmate.
  • For legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety and rights of our users.

Data retention and deletion

We keep account and booking information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app (Settings, Delete Account). Deleting your account permanently deletes your account and the personal data associated with it, except where we are required to keep limited records by law.

Your choices

  • You can view and update your profile and studio information in the app.
  • You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask for deletion, by contacting us at the email below.
  • California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA, including the right to know, delete, and not be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell personal information.

Children

Chairmate is intended for businesses and adult clients. Tattoo booking features are for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above.
